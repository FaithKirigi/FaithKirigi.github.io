FRAUD ANALYTICS · INVESTIGATION · RISK SCORING

Fraud Investigation Case Study

Investigating transaction behavior to identify fraud patterns, uncover suspicious activity, and separate higher-risk transactions from normal customer behavior.

Python Fraud Analytics Risk Scoring EDA

01 — THE CHALLENGE

Finding suspicious activity within mostly legitimate transactions

Fraud investigations often involve identifying a relatively small number of suspicious transactions within a much larger volume of legitimate activity.

The objective of this project was to investigate transaction behavior, determine which characteristics were most strongly associated with fraud, and develop a practical way of prioritizing transactions for further review.

02 — WHAT I DID

Investigated behavioral and transaction-level fraud indicators

I explored transaction characteristics including transaction amount, time of activity, merchant category, foreign transactions, location mismatches, device trust, transaction velocity, and cardholder characteristics.

I compared fraudulent and legitimate transactions to identify patterns associated with suspicious behavior and then used those findings to segment transactions into high-, medium-, and low-risk groups.

03 — KEY FINDINGS

What the investigation revealed

42.7% Fraud within the high-risk segment
3.3% Fraud within the medium-risk segment
0.01% Fraud within the low-risk segment

Behavioral signals were useful fraud indicators

Location mismatch, foreign transaction activity, transaction velocity, and device trust emerged as useful indicators for distinguishing suspicious behavior.

Fraud activity showed time-based patterns

The analysis identified increased fraudulent activity during overnight hours, particularly between midnight and early morning.

Risk segmentation concentrated suspicious activity

Fraud was substantially more concentrated in the high-risk segment than in the medium- and low-risk groups, providing a practical basis for prioritizing transactions for investigation.

04 — WHY IT MATTERS

Investigation teams need a way to prioritize limited review capacity

Reviewing every transaction with the same level of attention is inefficient. Risk segmentation can help investigators direct attention toward activity displaying multiple suspicious characteristics while allowing lower-risk transactions to receive proportionate treatment.

The analysis demonstrates how transaction and behavioral indicators can support fraud monitoring, investigation prioritization, and risk-based decision-making.
