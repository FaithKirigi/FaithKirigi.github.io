MSME Credit Risk Portfolio Analytics

Analyzing an MSME lending portfolio to understand borrower behavior, portfolio performance, credit risk, expected loss, and the factors associated with default.

Python Credit Risk ECL Machine Learning

01 — THE CHALLENGE

Understanding risk and return across an MSME lending portfolio

Lending decisions require more than understanding how much revenue a portfolio can generate. Lenders also need to understand borrower risk, expected losses, repayment behavior, and whether the portfolio generates sufficient returns for the level of credit exposure being taken.

I approached the project as a portfolio-level credit risk assessment, combining financial analysis, borrower segmentation, expected credit loss analysis, and predictive modelling.

02 — WHAT I DID

Combined portfolio analysis with borrower-level risk modelling

I analyzed the lending portfolio to understand loan exposure, interest income, expected credit losses, and overall financial contribution under a base-case scenario.

I also segmented borrowers using behavioral characteristics such as transaction frequency, transaction value, cash-flow volatility, and repayment behavior.

Finally, I developed a logistic regression model to estimate default risk and examine which borrower characteristics were most strongly associated with higher or lower risk.

03 — KEY FINDINGS

What the portfolio analysis revealed

$2.0M Portfolio exposure
$60K Base-case expected credit loss
0.837 Default model ROC-AUC

Portfolio profitability was sensitive to credit risk

The base-case analysis generated approximately $160,000 in monthly interest income, but expected credit losses reduced the portfolio's financial contribution to approximately $100,000.

Cash-flow volatility was associated with higher risk

Borrowers with more volatile cash-flow patterns were associated with a higher probability of default, making volatility an important behavioral risk signal.

Repayment behavior and activity helped distinguish risk

Higher transaction frequency and stronger repayment behavior were associated with lower default risk, demonstrating the value of behavioral information in borrower assessment.

04 — WHY IT MATTERS

Credit decisions require both risk and financial context

The project demonstrates how portfolio economics and borrower-level analytics can be considered together rather than treating credit risk as only a prediction problem.

Combining expected loss, borrower segmentation, behavioral indicators, and default modelling can help lenders identify higher-risk segments, improve monitoring, and make more informed credit decisions.
