About Me

Technically, I work primarily in Python and SQL, applying classification models and exploratory analysis to fraud detection problems. I hold certifications from Google, Microsoft, and WorldQuant in data analytics. I am particularly interested in roles where I can combine analytical thinking with technical skills to improve fraud detection systems and support smarter risk decisions
