About Me

Five years ago I started tracking money. First in logistics at Cossim, then in e-commerce operations at Jumia, then in data quality at CloudFactory. Every role had the same underlying question: where is something going wrong, and why isn't anyone catching it? That question eventually led me to fraud.

I'm now a Fraud Investigation Analyst at Sportserve, working with high-volume iGaming transaction data, flagging anomalies, investigating suspicious patterns, and building models that make the next investigation faster than the last.

My technical toolkit is Python, SQL, and machine learning. My actual job is making sure the right call gets made before the damage compounds.
