ABOUT ME

Turning data into practical insight

I am a data analyst focused on fraud, financial risk, transaction analytics, and business intelligence. I enjoy working with complex datasets to understand how activity behaves, identify meaningful patterns, and translate those findings into information that can support better decisions.

Through self-learning, hands-on projects, and professional experience, I have developed practical experience working across financial operations, fraud and risk analysis, and data analytics. This combination allows me to approach data not only from a technical perspective, but also with an understanding of the business and operational questions behind it.

My analytical toolkit includes SQL, Python, Excel, Power BI, Tableau, and statistical and machine-learning techniques. I am particularly interested in applying these skills to financial services, fintech, payments, credit risk, and business intelligence.
